Samarès Manor

Botanical gardens and a walled herb garden five minutes from town.

About this venue

A historic manor with botanical gardens, a walled herb garden and a Norman crypt chapel, five minutes from St Helier. The choice for a daytime summer event that should feel relaxed and green rather than corporate — garden receptions, team days and long-table lunches.

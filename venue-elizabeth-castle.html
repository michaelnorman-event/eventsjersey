Elizabeth Castle

A castle on its own tidal island, reached by causeway or amphibious ferry.

About this venue

A sixteenth-century fortress on an island that the sea cuts off twice a day. Guests arrive on foot across the causeway or by amphibious ferry, which means the journey is already the entertainment. Dinner goes up in a marquee on the parade ground.

Best for

  • Incentive dinners of 80–180
  • Summer parties with a spectacular arrival
  • Client hospitality that must out-do last year
  • Island-wide celebrations and anniversaries

Things to watch

  • Tides dictate the timetable — the schedule is built backwards from the last ferry
  • Everything, including the kitchen and generator, is shipped in
  • Not suitable if several guests have significant mobility needs

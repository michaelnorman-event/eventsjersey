Jersey War Tunnels

A kilometre of wartime tunnels at a constant 16°C, whatever the weather.

About this venue

Hand-dug German occupation tunnels cut into the hillside, hired after public hours. Genuinely unlike any other venue in the British Isles, weather-proof by definition, and it comes with a guided historical experience you can build into the programme.

  • Acoustics are live — amplified speech needs proper sound design
  • 16°C year round means guests want layers, even in August
  • Content is wartime history; brief hosts to pitch the tone right

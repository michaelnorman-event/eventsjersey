Five concepts

Jersey Royals, oysters, island dairy

Five catering concepts built around produce that comes off this island and the water around it. Menus are finalised four weeks out, against what's actually landing.

01 · Formal

The Island Banquet

Three or four courses · £68 – £115 pp

A proper seated dinner for awards nights and gala evenings. Jersey Royal velouté, line-caught brill, Jersey beef fillet, and a dessert built on island cream and honey.

  • Three or four courses with canapés on arrival
  • Half a bottle of wine per guest included
  • Service to 500 covers in a single wave
  • Vegan and vegetarian menus of equal standing
  • Menu tasting for the organiser included over 150 guests
02 · Seafood

Shellfish Market

Live stations · £55 – £85 pp

Manned market stalls instead of a plated meal: Royal Bay oysters shucked to order, chancre crab, local lobster, moules and hot-smoked mackerel. Guests circulate, which is exactly what you want at a networking dinner.

  • Four to six manned stations with chefs
  • Oysters shucked in front of guests
  • Non-shellfish and meat stations alongside
  • Pairs with Muscadet, island cider or stout
  • Works from 40 up to 300 guests
03 · Relaxed

Street-Food Festival

Truck village · £38 – £58 pp

Four to eight island food trucks and traders in a yard, courtyard or field, with picnic benches, festoon lighting and a bar container. The strongest format for family days, summer parties and all-staff events.

  • Island traders: wood-fired pizza, bao, barbecue, crêpes
  • Unlimited tokens or a two-item allowance per guest
  • Benches, festoon lighting and bar container included
  • Kids' portions for family-day formats
  • Works from 80 up to 1,000 guests
04 · Reception

Canapés & Island Cocktails

90 minutes to 3 hours · £32 – £62 pp

A standing reception for launches, client drinks and pre-dinner hours. Eight canapés per guest and a short cocktail list built on Jersey gin, island apple brandy and black butter.

  • Six to ten canapés per guest, served by staff
  • Three signature cocktails with island spirits
  • Mixologist station as a visible centrepiece
  • Strong zero-alcohol list, not an afterthought
  • Suits castle, theatre and tunnel venues
05 · Outdoor

Beach Barbecue & Oyster Bar

Sunset · £48 – £78 pp

Fire pits on the sand at St Ouen's or St Brelade's, whole fish and Jersey beef over coals, an oyster bar on ice, blankets as the sun goes down. The island at its best, and the thing delegates photograph.

  • Permitted beach sites with full licences held
  • Whole-fish, lobster and beef over open fire
  • Fire pits, blankets, rugs and lighting
  • Tide-timed; sunset slot confirmed at booking
  • Marquee or indoor fallback priced in advance
All day☕

Conference day catering

Add-on · £34 – £52 pp

The unglamorous essential: arrival pastries, two refreshment breaks with something warm, a working lunch that doesn't put the afternoon session to sleep, and an afternoon energy break.

  • Bowl-food or deli-bar lunch, not sandwiches in a box
  • Barista coffee option through the day
  • Dietary requirements labelled and plated separately

Questions

Catering FAQ

Can you cater at the castles and the War Tunnels?

Yes. None of the three heritage venues has a production kitchen, so we bring in a mobile kitchen and run service from a back-of-house area agreed with the site. It affects the menu — plated dinners are built around dishes that hold — and we flag that at proposal stage rather than on the night.

How do you handle allergens and dietary requirements?

A named list by guest ten days out, allergen matrix held by the head chef, separately plated dishes brought out by a designated server, and labelled stations on buffet formats. Vegan and vegetarian menus are written as proper menus, not an adapted side dish.

Is the produce genuinely local?

Where the island produces it, yes: Jersey Royals in season, island dairy and beef, oysters from the Royal Bay of Grouville, crab and lobster from island boats, and spirits from Jersey distillers. Out-of-season fruit and some store-cupboard items come in from the mainland, and we're straightforward about which is which.

Can we use our own caterer?

At the heritage and showground venues, yes — several approved caterers operate on-island and we'll contract whichever you prefer. Hotel venues almost always require their own kitchen; we negotiate the menu rather than the supplier there.

What does a bar package cost?

A three-hour reception package with wine, beer, two cocktails and soft drinks typically runs £28 – £45 per head. Cash bars, drinks tokens and consumption-to-a-cap arrangements all work; we recommend a cap with a named approver rather than an open tab.

Want a sample menu for your date?

Send us the format and the month. We'll build a menu around what will be in season and price it properly.

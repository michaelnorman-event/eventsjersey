Jersey Opera House

A restored Victorian theatre for plenaries, AGMs and ceremonies.

About this venue

A restored Victorian theatre in the middle of town, with tiered seating, a full lighting and sound rig and a proper stage. The right room when the content is the event — a keynote, an AGM, a product launch or an award ceremony that needs everyone facing the same way.

Best for

  • Product launches needing theatrical production

Things to watch

  • No seated dining — pair it with a dinner venue for the evening
  • Dates compete with the public programme; book early
  • Upper tiers are stairs only, so seat accessible guests in the stalls

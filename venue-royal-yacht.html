The Royal Yacht

Harbour-front four-star where delegates sleep, meet and dine in one building.

About this venue

A four-star hotel on the Weighbridge, two minutes from the finance district and the harbour. The practical default for a residential conference: bedrooms, meeting rooms, dining, a spa and three bars inside one footprint, so a two-day agenda never needs a coach.

  • Client drinks with a harbour backdrop

Things to watch

  • No dedicated car park — plan arrivals around the public multi-storey
  • The ballroom divides in two, so a 300-cover dinner uses the whole space with no breakout alongside
  • Weekend bedroom rates climb sharply in July and August

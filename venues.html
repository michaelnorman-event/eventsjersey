Ten venues

Every kind of room this island has

From a 500-cover waterfront ballroom to a drinks reception on a castle rampart with the tide coming in. Capacities are maximums for the stated layout and confirmed at proposal stage.

Filter
St Helier⚓

The Royal Yacht

Up to 300 · Waterfront hotel

Harbour-front four-star on the Weighbridge, a two-minute walk from the business district. Strong choice for conferences that need delegates sleeping, meeting and dining in one building.

  • Banqueting to 300; theatre to 250
  • 110 bedrooms plus spa on site
  • Six breakout rooms and a private dining room
  • Walking distance to the harbour and shops
St Helier🏛

Radisson Blu Waterfront Hotel

Up to 500 · Largest ballroom

The island's biggest single conference and banqueting space, purpose-built with pillar-free floor, loading access and full in-house AV. The default for large awards nights and multi-day conferences.

  • Pillar-free ballroom, banqueting to 500
  • Divisible into three separate suites
  • 195 bedrooms; marina and beach views
  • Exhibition-capable with vehicle access
St Brelade🌊

L'Horizon Beach Hotel & Spa

Up to 200 · Beachfront

Directly on St Brelade's Bay with the sand at the door. The island's best option for incentive groups and leadership offsites that want sea air between sessions.

  • Banqueting to 200; boardroom from 10
  • Beach-access terrace for receptions
  • Spa, pool and two restaurants
  • Fifteen minutes from the airport
St Helier✦

Grand Jersey Hotel & Spa

Up to 150 · Boutique five-star

Victorian seafront building with a modern interior and a Champagne lounge. Suits senior client hospitality and smaller gala dinners where the room needs to feel considered.

  • Banqueting to 150; private dining to 24
  • Champagne lounge for pre-dinner drinks
  • 123 bedrooms and a destination spa
  • Seafront position on Victoria Avenue
St Helier🎭

Jersey Opera House

Up to 600 theatre · Town centre

Restored Victorian theatre in the middle of town. Hire it for conference plenaries, AGMs, product launches and award ceremonies that want a proper stage and tiered seating.

  • Tiered auditorium seating to 600
  • Full theatrical lighting, sound and fly tower
  • Bars and foyer spaces for receptions
  • Green rooms for speakers and talent
Gorey🏰

Mont Orgueil Castle

Up to 120 reception · 13th century

A medieval fortress above Gorey harbour, lit at night and visible from the French coast. Used for drinks receptions and seated dinners in the Great Hall after public closing.

  • Reception to 120; seated dinner to 80
  • Exclusive use from 6pm, April to October
  • Ramparts with views to Normandy
  • Caterers brought in on a mobile kitchen
St Aubin's Bay⛴

Elizabeth Castle

Up to 250 · Tidal island fortress

A sixteenth-century castle on its own tidal island, reached by amphibious ferry or on foot across the causeway. The arrival is the entertainment; guests talk about it for years.

  • Reception to 250; marquee dinner to 180
  • Causeway or castle-ferry arrival by tide time
  • Parade ground for marquees and staging
  • Tide-dependent timings; we build the schedule
St Lawrence🕯

Jersey War Tunnels

Up to 150 · Underground

A kilometre of hand-dug wartime tunnels cut into the hillside. A genuinely singular space for evening receptions, team days with a guided element, and screenings.

  • Reception to 150; seated dinner to 90
  • Constant 16°C — weather-proof year round
  • Guided historical experience as a programme element
  • Projection-friendly tunnel galleries
Trinity⛺

Royal Jersey Showground

Up to 1,000 · Blank canvas

Open ground and exhibition halls in the north of the island. Where a Jersey event genuinely needs scale — company family days, summer festivals, exhibitions and marquee conferences.

  • Marquee capacity to 1,000 seated
  • Indoor halls plus large hard-standing
  • Power, water and on-site parking for 500 cars
  • Full build from an empty field
St Clement🌿

Samarès Manor

Up to 180 · Manor & gardens

A historic manor with botanical gardens, a walled herb garden and a Norman crypt chapel. Daytime summer hospitality, garden receptions and relaxed team days.

  • Garden reception to 180; marquee dinner to 140
  • Walled gardens and lakeside lawn
  • Herb garden tours and tastings on site
  • Five minutes from St Helier

Choosing

Quick guidance

Over 300 guests

Radisson Blu for a ballroom, Jersey Opera House for a ceremony with a stage, Royal Jersey Showground when you need to build it yourself.

A night they'll remember

Elizabeth Castle by ferry, Mont Orgueil's Great Hall, or the War Tunnels — all three are unavailable anywhere else in the British Isles.

Residential conference

The Royal Yacht or Radisson Blu keep delegates in one building; both sit inside the St Helier walking cluster for overflow bedrooms.

Leadership offsite

L'Horizon for sea air and a boardroom, Grand Jersey for discretion, Samarès Manor for a summer day session outdoors.

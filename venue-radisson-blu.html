Radisson Blu Waterfront Hotel

The island's largest pillar-free ballroom, purpose-built for conferences.

About this venue

Jersey's biggest single conference and banqueting space, built for the job: a pillar-free floor, vehicle access for exhibition build, in-house AV and 195 bedrooms upstairs. If the headcount is over 300 and the format is a ballroom, the conversation starts here.

Best for

Things to watch

  • The ballroom is in demand — book 12 months ahead for a Q4 awards date
  • Marina views are from the foyer and bedrooms, not the ballroom itself
  • Exhibition build needs an extra day's hire

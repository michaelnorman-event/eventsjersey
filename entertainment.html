Five programmes

The part of the agenda people remember

Five programmes that work on this island, with island suppliers we've used before. Every outdoor option ships with a tested wet-weather alternative.

01 · Music

Live music, scaled to the room

Drinks reception to late-night floor

A jazz trio through drinks, a string quartet for a formal dinner, or an eight-piece showband that fills the floor by 10pm. Island musicians plus mainland headliners when the budget allows.

  • Solo pianist, trio, quartet, or 8-piece band
  • DJ handover with island-legal curfew planning
  • Sound, lighting and a technician included
  • Acoustic sets ideal for castle and tunnel venues
  • From £750 (trio) to £6,500 (full showband)
02 · Active

Coastal adventure team-building

Half day · 8 – 80 guests

Jersey's coast is the asset: RIB racing out of St Catherine's, a skippered yacht regatta in St Aubin's Bay, coasteering off the north coast, or sea kayaking to Les Écréhous.

  • RIB challenge, sailing regatta, coasteering or kayaking
  • Qualified instructors, safety boats and all kit
  • Teams rotated so nobody sits out
  • Wet-weather swap: indoor escape challenge
  • From £95 per person
03 · Teams

The Island Challenge

3 – 4 hours · 12 – 200 guests

A GPS-scored treasure hunt across the parishes, run from tablets. Teams collect photo and video challenges at German bunkers, fishing harbours, the dolmen at La Hougue Bie and Rozel's slipway — then everyone watches the highlight reel over dinner.

  • Live scoreboard, app-based, no paper
  • Mini-coach, 2CV convoy or e-bike versions
  • Edited highlight film ready for the evening
  • Mixed-mobility routes available
  • From £65 per person
04 · Production

Awards night production

Evening · 100 – 500 guests

Full technical production for ceremonies: staging, screens, lighting states, a scripted running order, a professional host and an autocue that keeps your directors on time.

  • Stage, LED screen, lighting rig and sound
  • Host booking, from island names to UK comedians
  • Nomination films and sponsor idents produced
  • Rehearsal schedule and show-caller on the night
  • From £8,500 for a 250-guest ceremony
05 · Late night

Casino & cabaret evening

Evening · 40 – 300 guests

Fun-money roulette and blackjack with croupiers, close-up magicians working the tables through dinner, and an aerial or fire act for the reveal moment. Charity-auction format available.

  • Roulette, blackjack and poker tables with croupiers
  • Close-up magic and caricaturists during dinner
  • Aerial, fire or LED speciality act
  • Prize structure and charity-auction option
  • From £3,200 for 150 guests

Contingency

The weather plan is written before you sign

Jersey weather turns quickly. Every outdoor element in our proposals carries a named alternative, a decision deadline and a cost position, so nobody is making calls on the day.

  • A named indoor alternative for each outdoor element
  • A decision point, usually 18 hours ahead, agreed in advance
  • Marquee, heating and flooring options priced upfront
  • Tide tables factored into castle and beach timings
  • Supplier cancellation terms disclosed in the proposal

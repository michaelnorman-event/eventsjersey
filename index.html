Why Jersey

A whole island that works like one event campus

Nothing in Jersey is more than twenty minutes away. Delegates land at 10am, are in a plenary session by 11, on a RIB off St Catherine's by 4, and at dinner in a castle by 7 — with no coach transfers eating the agenda.

  • Direct flights from London, Manchester, Birmingham, Dublin and Paris
  • Finance-sector infrastructure: AV, connectivity and conference capacity
  • Walkable St Helier hotel cluster for residential conferences
  • Heritage sites, beaches and country estates for off-site evenings
  • One on-island team, one contract, one point of contact

How it works

From brief to run sheet in four steps

Brief

A 30-minute call covering numbers, dates, budget band and the outcome you need from the event.

Proposal

Within 48 hours: three venue options, a programme, menus and a costed budget with no hidden extras.

Build

Contracting, site visits, AV specification, transport, staffing, rooming lists and a minute-by-minute run sheet.

Deliver

Getting here

Jersey Airport is a 45-minute flight from London Gatwick and City, with direct routes from Manchester, Birmingham, Southampton, Dublin and Paris. Condor Ferries sail from Poole and Portsmouth. The airport is twelve minutes from St Helier.

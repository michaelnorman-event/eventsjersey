Royal Jersey Showground

Open ground and halls for the events that genuinely need scale.

About this venue

Exhibition halls and open ground in the north of the island, with power, water and parking for 500 cars. This is where a Jersey event stops fitting in a hotel: family days, summer festivals, exhibitions and marquee conferences built from an empty field.

Best for

  • Company family days and all-staff events of 300–1,500
  • Summer festivals and street-food formats
  • Exhibitions and trade shows
  • Conferences with no hotel large enough

Things to watch

  • Everything is built from scratch — budget for marquee, flooring, power and WCs
  • Build and break typically need two extra days
  • Ground conditions after heavy rain affect vehicle access
